# Codex Execution Brief — Complete Scrap AI as a Local + Global Scrap Platform

You are taking full ownership of completing Scrap AI as a polished Saudi-first scrap valuation, pickup and marketplace product.

Repository: https://github.com/majaber1/scrap-ai

Primary experience benchmark: https://www.thekabadiwala.com/individual
International B2B feature reference: https://scrapad.com/en/

## Non-negotiable positioning

Scrap AI is an independent platform for:
- Saudi individuals who need the easiest possible way to value and sell scrap with pickup;
- Saudi companies that need lots, tenders/auctions, scheduled collections, compliance evidence and recycling reports;
- verified local recyclers and scrap buyers;
- verified international B2B buyers for commercially viable aggregated/export lots.

It is not the EADA/ReExport Saudi platform and must not sell ordinary imported surplus or manufactured Saudi products.

Use The Kabadiwala as the simplicity benchmark: choose/identify material, schedule pickup, verified collector, transparent weighing and payment. Use ScrapAd only as the reference for verified international scrap trading, secure commercial workflow and logistics. Create original branding, UI and content.

## Phase 1 goal

Deliver a complete presentation-ready workflow from photo intake to indicative AI valuation, competing offers or pickup, verified weighing, final price, payment simulation and completed recycling transaction. Include a separate business/global workflow without making the ordinary customer interface complicated.

Defer certified AI accuracy, live payments, advanced KYC, live environmental/government integrations, real export settlement, penetration testing and unnecessary microservices to Phase 2. Label simulations and estimates clearly.

## Core individual journey — maximum simplicity

The default home CTA is “صوّر السكراب واعرف قيمته”.

1. Take/upload 1–5 photos.
2. AI-demo classification suggests material, purity/condition and approximate weight; ask only simple follow-up questions.
3. Show a clear indicative price range and what affects it.
4. Get location and pickup preference.
5. Choose instant pickup offer or receive nearby verified-buyer offers.
6. Confirm time and see assigned verified collector.
7. Track pickup.
8. Record digital-scale gross/tare/net weight with photo evidence.
9. Recalculate final price and ask customer confirmation.
10. Simulate cashless payment and issue receipt plus environmental-impact summary.

Guest estimation is allowed. Require phone/account only when booking pickup or accepting an offer. Never force an individual to create a formal marketplace listing.

## Local and global market structure

Local marketplace:
- individuals, companies, licensed/verified collectors, yards and recyclers;
- small pickup requests and commercial lots;
- distance, availability, ratings, accepted materials and price basis.

Global marketplace:
- B2B only;
- only aggregated/commercially viable lots;
- verified seller and buyer companies;
- material standard/grade, contamination, quantity tolerance, packing, origin, port, Incoterms, inspection, documents, payment protection simulation and logistics milestones;
- restricted/hazardous materials remain blocked pending required approvals.

Individuals never ship directly to an international buyer. Their material may be aggregated by a verified operator and later offered as a separate commercial lot.

## Material coverage

Support metals (copper, aluminum, steel/iron, brass, stainless, lead where eligible), cables, batteries with restriction states, e-waste, appliances, paper/cardboard, plastics, glass, tires/rubber, auto parts, machinery and construction/industrial scrap. Every category needs units, typical grade questions, contamination rules, pickup feasibility and disclaimer.
